# TrackTag User Manual

## 1. Product Overview

TrackTag is a compact smart tracking device designed to help locate personal belongings such as keys, bags, wallets, luggage, and other valuable items.

## 2. Getting Started

1. Remove the TrackTag from its packaging.
2. Activate the device using the power button.
3. Turn on Bluetooth on the smartphone.
4. Open the TrackTag application.
5. Follow the on-screen instructions to pair the device.

## 3. Connecting TrackTag

1. Keep TrackTag close to the smartphone.
2. Enable Bluetooth and location services.
3. Open the application and select **Add Device**.
4. Select TrackTag from the available devices.
5. Complete the pairing process.

## 4. Finding Your Device

Open the application and select the connected TrackTag. The application displays its last known location. If the device is nearby, use the **Play Sound** option to help locate it.

## 5. Lost Mode

Enable **Lost Mode** when TrackTag is missing. The device can be monitored through its last known location, allowing the user to identify where it was last detected.

## 6. Battery

TrackTag uses a replaceable battery. Replace the battery when the device stops responding or the battery level becomes low.

## 7. Safety Instructions

* Keep the device away from water unless it is specifically rated as waterproof.
* Do not expose the device to extreme temperatures.
* Keep the battery away from children.
* Do not attempt to damage or open the electronic components.

## 8. Troubleshooting

**TrackTag is not connecting:**
Check that Bluetooth and location services are enabled and keep the device close to the smartphone.

**Location is not updating:**
Move the device to an area with better connectivity and reopen the application.

**TrackTag is not making a sound:**
Check the battery and ensure the device is connected to the application.

## 9. Support

For additional assistance, check the TrackTag application or contact the product support team.
